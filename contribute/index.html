Contribute

This guide has contributors. Here are five ways to be one.

The best coffee advice comes from people who drink a lot of coffee. This guide gets better with every reader who adds to it. Pick a way to pitch in.

Review a place

Been to a roaster or café we cover, or one we haven't found yet? Send a short, honest review. Good or critical, as long as it's fair and from a real visit.

Write a review

Send a full visit package

The best contributions show what a place is really like. If you visited, send photos and, if you can, an interview with the owner or manager. We'll build it into a profile or a feature, and credit you.

What to send:

  • Photos. Take them yourself or have permission from whoever did. Include the storefront, the room, the coffee, and the roasting or bar if you can. Send full-size files, with a short caption for each. Please don't send photos where a person can be identified unless they agreed.
  • An interview, if you have one. A transcript or notes from a conversation with the owner, manager, or roaster. Tell us the date, the person's name and role, and that they know it may be published. Keep it in their own words, with the questions and answers as they happened.
  • Your own impressions. What the place felt like, how it smelled, who was there, what you ordered. Honest and specific beats glowing.
  • The basics. Name, street address, website, and the day you visited. We only use hours as the business posts them, so send them from its own site or door sign, with the date.

Recommend a place

Know a roaster or café we should cover? Tell us its name, its street address, and why it's worth a stop. The address helps us put a map pin on it. If it fits, we'll research it, write it up, and thank you for the tip.

Share a brewing tip

Got a ratio, a method, or a fix for a bad cup? Send it. We check tips against other sources before they go into a guide, and we credit you when one does.

Tell a coffee story

A diner you almost drove past, a roaster that changed how you drink coffee, a morning you remember. Short and true. Smells, sounds, and the people count.

Tell a story

If a button doesn't open your email, send it to info@wickedgoodcoffee.com and tell us which kind of contribution it is.

Ground rules

  • It's free. There's no cost to contribute and no payment for publishing. We don't pay for reviews or tips, and we don't trade free coffee for them.
  • Keep it real. Write about things you actually know or did. Atmosphere and impressions are welcome, as long as you were there. If you own, work for, or compete with a place, say so.
  • Photos and interviews need permission. Send only photos you took or have the right to share, and interviews the person agreed could be published. You keep your copyright, and you let us use them on this site and in the newsletter with a credit.
  • Addresses help. For a place recommendation or review, a street address lets us drop a map pin that opens correctly in Google and Apple Maps. If you don't have it, send what you have.
  • Credit is yours to choose. We thank you by the first name and town you give us, or anonymously. We never publish your email address.
  • We check before we publish. Place tips and brewing tips get verified against other sources. We may edit for length and clarity, and we won't change what you meant.
  • We can't publish everything. Personal attacks, accusations we can't support, spam, and ads are out.
  • Your permission. By sending something, you let us publish it on this site and in the newsletter.
