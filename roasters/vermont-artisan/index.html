Vermont Artisan Coffee & Tea in Waterbury Center, Vermont

A Vermont roaster in a converted barn, with a coffee bar and a wood-fired roaster.

Waterbury Center, VT

Barn roastery and coffee bar. 11 Cabin Lane, Waterbury Center, VT 05677

Waterbury Center
VT

Vermont Artisan Coffee & Tea roasts in a converted barn in Waterbury Center, next door to a coffee testing lab and a school of coffee. The roastery sells beans online and runs a coffee bar on site, and one of its roasters is fired with wood.

The short version

Where
11 Cabin Lane, Waterbury Center, VT 05677. Phone 802-244-8338.
Started
2001, per Seven Days and Daily Coffee News
Owner
Mané Alves, a Portugal native and former California winemaker, per Seven Days
Hours
Monday to Thursday 8am to 3pm, Friday to Sunday 8am to 4pm, as posted on its site on October 7, 2026
Visit
A coffee bar in the barn, plus roastery tours with tastings listed on its site

The roastery

Per Daily Coffee News (2019), the 15,000 square foot barn, built in 2017, holds six roasters ranging from 3 kg to 90 kg, including a wood-fired 15 kg Ghibli. It sits beside Coffee Lab International, a testing lab, and the CLI School of Coffee. Seven Days reports that the move to Waterbury Center followed flood damage from Tropical Storm Irene in 2011.

How it sources

The company's about page describes "Farm Direct" sourcing and says many of its coffees are certified Organic and Fair Trade. It also lists sustainability practices.

Where to start

Its shop lists Artisan Blend and Breakfast Blend as house blends. A blend is the easy way into any new roaster.

Recognition

Per Seven Days, Vermont Artisan was a finalist for Best Coffee Roaster in the 2023 Seven Daysies.

This profile is researched from published sources, not from a visit. Hours and offerings change, so check before you go. Sources: Vermont Artisan, its about page, Seven Days, and Daily Coffee News.
