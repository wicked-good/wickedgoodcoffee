Roaster profile, Vermont, October 2026

Speeder & Earl's Coffee in Burlington, Vermont

A Burlington, Vermont roaster and cafe going since 1993.

Speeder & Earl's has been a Burlington coffee name since 1993. The cafe is on Pine Street, and the roasting happens at a separate facility in Williston that isn't open to the public.

The short version

Cafe
412 Pine Street, Burlington, VT 05401. Phone (802) 658-6016.
Roastery
Williston, Vermont. Not open to the public.
Started
1993. Per Seven Days (2012), founded by Jessica Workman and Jeannie Vento. Its site calls the company family owned and doesn't name owners, so current ownership isn't confirmed here.
Hours
Not confirmed. Check its site before you go.

The history

Per Seven Days (2012), roasting began in the back of the Pine Street cafe. At the time the company produced about 7,000 to 10,000 pounds a month and was moving roasting to a new 5,000 square foot facility in Williston. Its site says it has been operating for more than 30 years.

Where to start

Its shop lists Speeder's Blend and a Maple French Roast, which the company calls iconic. It also sells single-origin, fair-trade organic, and flavored coffees. The Pine Street cafe offers local pickup and online ordering.

Recognition

Per Seven Days, Speeder & Earl's won Best Coffee Roaster in the 2023 Seven Daysies.

This profile is researched from published sources, not from a visit. Hours and offerings change, so check before you go. Sources: Speeder & Earl's, Seven Days (2012), and Seven Daysies 2023.
