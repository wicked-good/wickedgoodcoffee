Roaster profile, Vermont, October 2026

Brio Coffeeworks in Burlington, Vermont

A Burlington, Vermont roaster with a coffee bar, an equipment shop, and a big wholesale business.

Brio Coffeeworks is a roastery, coffee bar, and equipment shop in Burlington's South End Arts District. Most of its business is wholesale to cafes, restaurants, and markets, and it keeps a wide range of coffees on hand.

The short version

Where
266 Pine Street, Suite 116, Burlington, VT 05401. Phone 802-503-4233.
Started
2014, by Nate and Magda Van Dusen, who took over Black River Roasters' Vermont operation that May, per Sprudge
Ownership
Its site calls it a woman-owned specialty roastery
Hours
Monday to Friday 8am to 3pm, Saturday and Sunday 8am to 4pm, as posted on its site on October 7, 2026

What it does

Per Seven Days, Brio focuses on wholesale, and keeps roughly 10 to 20 coffees on hand. Its range includes single origins, blends, espresso, decaf, and a series of microlots with artist collaborations, according to its site. It also caters, including nitro cold brew.

Where to start

Its shop lists Belle Epoque, a blend, among its coffees. As with any new roaster, a blend is the easy way in.

Recognition

Per Seven Days, Brio was a finalist for Best Coffee Roaster in the 2023 Seven Daysies.

This profile is researched from published sources, not from a visit. Hours and offerings change, so check before you go. Sources: Brio Coffeeworks, Sprudge, and Seven Days.
