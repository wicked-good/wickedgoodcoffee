Counter Culture Coffee: a national roaster with a Boston-area training center

A national roaster with a Boston-area training center and a lot of published detail about how it buys coffee.

Counter Culture Coffee is a national roaster based in Durham, North Carolina. It's included here because it has a training center in Somerville, Massachusetts, and because it publishes more about how it buys coffee than most.

The short version

Headquarters
Durham, North Carolina
Founded
1995, by Brett Smith and Fred Houk, per the company and the Daily Tar Heel
New England
A training center at 374 Somerville Avenue, Somerville, MA 02143. It's a training center, not a cafe, and its site posts no hours.
Leadership
Joe Prewett became CEO on August 1, 2025, with Brett Smith moving to Executive Chairman, per GCR Magazine

What it publishes

Per the company's history page, Counter Culture:

  • Earned organic certification in 2002, which it says was the first for a North Carolina roaster
  • Set a Direct Trade policy in 2008
  • Started a Seeds program in 2010 that gives money to coffee-producing communities
  • Became B Corp certified in 2020
  • Publishes a Transparency Report

Where to start

Its homepage lists Big Trouble, Hologram, and Forty-Six among its coffees.

This profile is researched from published sources, not from a visit. Locations and offerings change, so check before you go. Sources: Counter Culture, its history page, its training centers page, and GCR Magazine.
