Roasters

Who's roasting well and what they're known for. We start in New England and go wider.

Maine, wood-fired

Speckled Ax

One of a handful of wood-fired coffee roasters in the United States, in Portland, Maine.

Waterbury Center, Vermont

Vermont Artisan Coffee & Tea

A barn roastery with a coffee bar, six roasters, and one that's fired with wood.

Burlington, Vermont

Brio Coffeeworks

A South End roaster with a coffee bar, an equipment shop, and a long list of coffees.

Burlington, Vermont

Speeder & Earl's Coffee

A Burlington roaster and Pine Street cafe, going since 1993.

Portland, Maine

Coffee By Design

A Portland roaster and cafe since 1994, with a fund that backs social-change art.

Rockland, Maine

Rock City Coffee Roasters

A Midcoast Maine cafe and roaster owned by its employees as a cooperative.

Acton, Massachusetts

George Howell Coffee

A light-roast, single-estate roaster with cafes around Boston and a long place in American coffee.

Providence, Rhode Island

The Coffee Exchange

A Wickenden Street roaster and cafe since 1984, buying most of its beans through a cooperative.

Durham, North Carolina

Counter Culture Coffee

A national roaster with a Boston-area training center and a published sourcing record.
