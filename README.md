# wickedgoodcoffee

Static site for wickedgoodcoffee.com. Plain HTML and CSS, no build step.

## Editorial rules (apply to every new page and email)

- Measurements always give both units: grams with ounces in parentheses, water in grams with fluid ounces, temperatures in Fahrenheit.
- No first-person taste claims. Nothing says someone tasted, drank, or visited unless that actually happened. Recommendations are researched and say what they are based on.
- The About page says content is a mix of research, personal visits, and reader contributions, and every page states its basis. Never claim a visit, tasting, or experience that didn't happen. A page says it reflects a visit only when Glenn actually went.
- Never use em dashes (the long dash) in copy, on the site or in emails. Use commas, periods, colons, or parentheses.
- Byline, when one is needed: "Glenn, a wicked pissah guy" (first name only).
- Brand: espresso #2A1811, roasted wood #6B371F, cream #F4E8D2, gold #B88746 (decorative only), iron #1C1A18. Libre Baskerville headlines, Lora body.

## Reader reviews

Reviews arrive at info@wickedgoodcoffee.com. Publish only ones that follow the rules on /reviews/: a real visit, no payment or free product, honest and fair, no personal attacks or accusations we can't support. Publish good and critical reviews alike. Label them as reader reviews, show only the name and town the reader gave (or "anonymous"), never their email, and keep them separate from our researched profiles.

## Contributions

/contribute/ takes reader reviews, place tips, brewing tips, and coffee stories by email to info@wickedgoodcoffee.com. Reviews follow the rules above. Place tips and brewing tips are verified against other sources before use, and the contributor is thanked by the first name and town they gave (or anonymously). Never publish a contributor's email. List credited contributors in the Contributors section of /contribute/.

## Building the site

Pages are generated by `_build/build.py` (which loads `_build/content2.py`). Run `python3 -I _build/build.py` from the repo root and commit the output. To add a roaster or cafe, add a `place(...)` call and an `EXTRA_R` or `EXTRA_S` row at the end of the matching block in `_build/content2.py` (copy an existing profile as the pattern). To add a guide, add a `guide(...)` call and a row in `_G`. Then regenerate `sitemap.xml` (one `<url>` per page, `lastmod` set to today for new or changed pages).

## Weekly routine

One new roaster or cafe profile per week, plus the matching newsletter draft in beehiiv. Facts must be verified against the company's own site plus an independent source, hours only as posted with the date checked, and anything unverified is left out or flagged. Newsletter drafts copy the design of issue 1 (beehiiv post `post_dbf735b3-baff-45e2-a290-d5a5f11a34e3`, use `duplicate_post`) and are never scheduled or sent by the routine. Glenn schedules them for Thursday at 8:00am Eastern.
