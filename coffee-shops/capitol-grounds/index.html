Capitol Grounds Café in Montpelier, Vermont

A Montpelier, Vermont coffee shop on State Street, open since 1998.

Capitol Grounds is a coffee shop on State Street in Montpelier, Vermont, a few steps from the capital's main street. It has been there since 1998.

The short version

Where
27 State Street, Montpelier, VT
Opened
1998, per Seven Days
Hours and phone
Not confirmed. Check capitolgrounds.com before you go.

The roaster connection

Per Seven Days (2017), the shop's roasting side rebranded as 802 Coffee on March 20, 2017, and later moved to East Montpelier, while the cafe kept the Capitol Grounds name.

The cafe

A 2014 Seven Days review described a parklet out front designed by Vermont Tech students and a menu built around local ingredients, including chili, sandwiches, and quiche. That review is more than a decade old, so the menu has likely changed.

Recognition

Capitol Grounds is listed in the 2026 Seven Daysies results for best coffee shop.

This profile is researched from published sources, not from a visit. Hours and menus change, so check before you go. Sources: Capitol Grounds, Seven Days (2017), Seven Days (2014 review), and Seven Daysies 2026.
