Subculture Coffee at Harbourside Place in Jupiter, Florida

A waterfront Subculture Coffee shop in Jupiter, Florida, with hand-drawn murals, egg chairs, and a magenta drum roaster on the floor.

Jupiter, FL

Harbourside Place. 107 Front Street, Jupiter, FL 33477

This is the third Subculture Coffee Roasters shop that has landed on this site, after the original on Clematis Street in West Palm Beach and the Delray Beach shop. The Jupiter location sits inside Harbourside Place, a waterfront mixed-use development on the Intracoastal, next to Tommy Bahama and The Jupiter Grill. It roasts on site.

The short version

Where
107 Front Street, Jupiter, FL 33477, in Harbourside Place
Hours
Sunday to Thursday 7am to 8pm, Wednesday open to 9pm for trivia night, Friday and Saturday 7am to 10pm, per the shop's Harbourside Place listing
What's on
Hot and iced espresso drinks, drip, slow pour, French press, flavored lattes, cold brew, affogato
Kitchen
Avocado toast, acai bowls, smoothies, house-made quiche, empanadas, breakfast burritos, bacon-and-egg and ham-and-swiss croissants
Equipment
La Marzocco espresso machine, Tri-Mist drum roaster in magenta, on the floor behind the counter
Coffee
Subculture's own, roasted here in small batches

Each Subculture is different

Three shops in now and the pattern is clear: Subculture leans into the character of each room, rather than stamping one template across the group. Clematis Street in West Palm Beach is the roastery flagship. Delray Beach is a low geometric block of color with palm trees out front and 302 above the door. The Jupiter shop is a waterfront room full of eclectic furniture and hand-drawn murals, with the roaster right there on the floor.

The room

White subway tile floor to ceiling. A burgundy ceiling, lit by track lights and the odd pendant. The espresso counter is a La Marzocco in matte white, stickered along the top with a row of tiny pixel-art creatures. Behind the counter, five hand-illustrated menu boards cover hot, iced, breakfast, flavored lattes, and a flat-out trippy "Try Us Or Report Us" board with a cartoon spaceman. On the wall opposite, a full mural of steampunk coffee drawings: skulls with cameras, a zeppelin, a rocket, a grinder, a cup. Signed CPG.

Seating is mixed on purpose: brown leather egg chairs around a small marble bistro table on the floor, booths along the mural wall, bar seating at the window. An embossed-tin counter column stands in front of a floor-to-ceiling wallpaper of black-and-white mid-century celebrity photos.

The roaster on the floor

The thing you walk around to see is the Tri-Mist drum roaster in magenta, sitting on an open platform inside the cafe itself, against a wall of mid-century black-and-white photos. On the day Glenn visited, a plush pink octopus was draped over the drum. Inside the hopper was a pile of small plush animals. A sign on the wall behind read COFFEE, and a small chalk sketch of a stressed coffee bean on a cup.

Something was happening

On the day Glenn visited, the shop was also staging a silent auction of framed celebrity memorabilia (Taylor Swift, Johnny Cash, a "Man in the Arena" plaque) benefiting Families First of Palm Beach County. The kind of event that, from the outside, does not look like a coffee shop thing to be doing. The kind of event that, in Subculture's rooms, fits.

Where to start

Order the drink of the week off the small chalkboard next to the espresso machine, or a slow pour if the counter isn't swamped. Add an empanada or a breakfast sandwich. Pick the seat that fits the mood: window bar, booth under the mural, egg chairs by the roaster. Then wander the room.

This profile is based on Glenn's visit to Subculture Coffee at Harbourside Place on November 17, 2026. Menu items, equipment and the silent-auction event are from photos taken on the same visit. Address and hours are as posted on the shop's listing at Harbourside Place. See also the Subculture roaster profile and the Delray shop profile, both linked above.
