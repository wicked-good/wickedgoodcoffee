Gracenote Coffee in Boston, Massachusetts

A small Boston coffee bar from a roastery that started in 2012, with a second spot that stays open late.

Gracenote is a tiny Boston coffee bar from a roastery that started in 2012. It's a standing-room-only shop in the Financial District, with a second location nearby that stays open into the evening and serves wine and cocktails.

The short version

Where
108 Lincoln Street, Boston, MA 02111
Hours
Monday to Friday 7am to 4pm, Saturday and Sunday 9am to 3pm, as posted on its site on October 7, 2026
Opened
October 2015, per Boston Magazine
Owners
Per Boston Magazine (2015), Alessandro "San" Bellino and Patrick Barter. Current involvement isn't confirmed here.
Second location
100 High Street Place, Boston. Monday to Wednesday 7am to 8pm, Thursday and Friday 7am to 10pm, Saturday 9am to 10pm, closed Sunday.

The shop

Per Boston Magazine, the original cafe is 240 square feet, with an olive live-edge standing bar and a Modbar espresso machine. Brian's Coffee Spot describes it as standing-room-only, with bright corner windows. The roastery dates to 2012 in Berlin, Massachusetts, per Brian's Coffee Spot.

The High Street location

The High Street Place cafe opened in March 2022 and serves wine and cocktails in the evening, per Brian's Coffee Spot.

This profile is researched from published sources, not from a visit. Hours and menus change, so check before you go. Sources: Gracenote, Boston Magazine, Brian's Coffee Spot (2016), and Brian's Coffee Spot (2022).
